Have you ever wondered how airports keep track of time and ensure that flights depart and arrive on schedule? The answer lies in the airport master clock, a precision timekeeping system that plays a critical role in air travel. Learn more about how these clocks work and their importance in keeping passengers safe.

## **What are airport master clocks?**

[](https://github.com/inalix/website-hugo/blob/test/content/english/news/master-clock-airport.md#what-are-airport-master-clocks)

Airport master clocks are precision timekeeping systems that synchronize all clocks throughout an airport, including those in terminals, control towers, and on the airfield. These clocks are typically connected to a GPS system to ensure accuracy and are programmed to adjust for daylight saving time and other time changes. The master clock system helps ensure that all airport operations are synchronized and on time, which is critical for safe and efficient air travel.

## **How do airport master clocks work?**

[](https://github.com/inalix/website-hugo/blob/test/content/english/news/master-clock-airport.md#how-do-airport-master-clocks-work)

Airport master clocks work by receiving a signal from a GPS system that provides the exact time and coordinates of the airport. This signal is then used to synchronize all clocks throughout the airport, including those in terminals, control towers, and on the airfield. The master clock system also adjusts for daylight saving time and other time changes, ensuring that all airport operations are synchronized and on time. This precision timekeeping is critical for safe and efficient air travel, as it helps ensure that planes take off and land on schedule and that passengers arrive at their destinations safely and on time.

## **Importance of accurate timekeeping in air travel.**

[](https://github.com/inalix/website-hugo/blob/test/content/english/news/master-clock-airport.md#importance-of-accurate-timekeeping-in-air-travel)

Accurate timekeeping is crucial in air travel for several reasons. First, it helps ensure that planes take off and land on schedule, which is essential for maintaining efficient operations and minimizing delays. Second, it helps ensure that air traffic controllers can coordinate the movements of planes in the air and on the ground, reducing the risk of collisions and other safety hazards. Finally, accurate timekeeping is important for passenger safety, as it helps ensure that planes are properly maintained and serviced according to schedule. Overall, airport master clocks play a critical role in keeping air travel safe and on time.
