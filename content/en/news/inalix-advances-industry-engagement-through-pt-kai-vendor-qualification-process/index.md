**INALIX recently engaged with the Procurement Team of PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero) as part of the vendor qualification process**, marking an important step in strengthening industry relationships and exploring potential business opportunities within Indonesia's transportation infrastructure sector.

The visit provided an opportunity for INALIX to present its company profile, technical capabilities, project experience, and portfolio of technology solutions. Through direct discussions, both teams exchanged insights and explored how INALIX's expertise could support PT KAI's operational and technological requirements.

With extensive experience in developing and implementing integrated technology systems, particularly in airport operations and industrial infrastructure, INALIX continues to expand its capabilities to address the evolving needs of various strategic industries.

This engagement represents INALIX's ongoing efforts to extend its expertise beyond the aviation sector, strengthen its industry network, and contribute to the development of reliable, efficient, and scalable technology solutions across Indonesia's transportation ecosystem.

**INALIX remains committed to fostering strategic industry relationships, driving technological innovation, and exploring meaningful opportunities for long-term collaboration.**
