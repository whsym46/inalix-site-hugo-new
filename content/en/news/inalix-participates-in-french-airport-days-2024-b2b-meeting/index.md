INALIX was honored to receive an invitation from the Embassy of France to attend the **French Airport Days 2024 B2B Meeting**, held on December 4, 2024, at Pullman Hotel.

The event served as a platform for business networking and knowledge exchange, bringing together industry stakeholders to explore opportunities for collaboration within the aviation and airport infrastructure sectors.

As an Indonesian technology company specializing in airport solutions, INALIX welcomed the opportunity to engage with international industry professionals, exchange insights, and explore potential partnerships that support the advancement of airport technology and operational efficiency.

Through its portfolio of airport technology solutions, including Flight Information Display Systems (FIDS), Airport Operational Database (AODB), and Airport Announcement Systems (AAS), INALIX remains committed to delivering innovative, reliable, and integrated solutions for the aviation industry.

Participation in French Airport Days 2024 reflects INALIX's continued efforts to strengthen industry relationships, expand its international network, and explore new opportunities for technological collaboration.

**INALIX looks forward to building meaningful partnerships and contributing to the future of smarter, more connected airport operations.**
