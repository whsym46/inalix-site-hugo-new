**INALIX Employee Gathering 2026** took place from August 25 to 28, 2026, in Lembang, Bandung, bringing team members together for four days of connection, shared experiences, and memorable moments beyond the workplace.

Set against the refreshing natural surroundings of Lembang, the gathering provided an opportunity for employees to step away from their daily routines, strengthen personal connections, and build a greater sense of unity across the organization.

More than just a company gathering, the event reflected INALIX's commitment to fostering a positive, inclusive, and collaborative workplace culture. By creating opportunities for meaningful interaction outside the professional environment, INALIX aims to encourage stronger communication, mutual trust, and teamwork among its people.

At INALIX, we believe that sustainable business growth is driven not only by technological innovation and operational excellence but also by the people behind every achievement. A strong and connected team serves as the foundation for delivering reliable solutions, overcoming challenges, and continuously moving forward together.

The Employee Gathering 2026 served as a reminder of the importance of collaboration, shared values, and a collective commitment to the company's vision.

**With stronger connections and renewed team spirit, INALIX looks forward to continuing its journey of innovation, collaboration, and shared success. Together, we grow stronger.**
