At INALIX, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data that you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, processing, and protection when you visit our website or interact with our services.

### 1. Information We Collect
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Fill out a contact inquiry or business consultation form (such as your name, corporate email, phone number, and company name).
- Communicate with our team via email or messaging channels.
- Subscribe to our newsletters or system updates.

Additionally, our website may automatically collect standard log information, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited, through analytics cookies.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect for legitimate business purposes, including:
- Responding to your inquiries regarding our airport and industrial systems.
- Providing customer support, product demos, and technical consultations.
- Improving our website performance, security, and user experience.
- Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.

### 3. Data Protection and Security
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel who require it to perform their official duties.

### 4. Disclosure to Third Parties
INALIX does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may only disclose your information:
- To trusted service providers and contractors who assist us in operating our website or conducting business, under strict confidentiality agreements.
- When required by law, regulation, or legal proceedings.

### 5. Cookies
Our website uses essential and analytics cookies to enhance user experience and understand how visitors interact with our content. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, though some features of the website may not function properly without them.

### 6. Your Rights
Depending on applicable data privacy laws, you have the right to access, correct, update, or request the deletion of your personal data held by us. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

### 7. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. The updated policy will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at:
