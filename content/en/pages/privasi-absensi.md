### 1. Introduction
This application is an internal application used by employees within the company environment. This privacy policy explains how personal data is collected, used, and protected in the use of this application.

### 2. Information Collected
We may collect the following information when you use the application:
- User identity data (name, email, employee number, position, and department)
- In-app activity data
- Device information (device type, operating system, IP address, and other technical data)

### 3. Use of Information
The collected information is used to:
- Manage user access and authentication
- Monitor and improve application performance
- Provide technical and operational support
- Ensure compliance with company policies and regulations

### 4. Data Protection
We are committed to maintaining the security of user data with the following measures:
- Encryption of data when stored and transmitted
- Access restrictions based on user authorization
- Periodic system monitoring and audits

### 5. Information Sharing
We will not share user information with third parties without permission, unless required by law or company policy.

### 6. User Rights
Users have the right to:
- Access and update their personal information
- Request deletion of data in accordance with company policy
- Submit complaints regarding data privacy and security

### 7. Policy Changes
This policy may be updated at any time. Any changes will be informed to users through the application or the company's internal communication media.

### 8. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the company's IT or HR department via the official company email.
