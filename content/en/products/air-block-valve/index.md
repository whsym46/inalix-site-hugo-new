An Air Block Valve is a component used in industrial air distribution and control systems, designed to open, close, or isolate airflow according to operational requirements. It is commonly applied in industrial installations where controlled air supply and reliable isolation are essential.

Designed for pneumatic systems and industrial utilities, the Air Block Valve helps maintain equipment operation and simplifies maintenance procedures by allowing airflow to be isolated in specific sections of a system.

**Key Features**

- Designed for airflow isolation and control.
- Suitable for compressed air distribution systems.
- Facilitates system isolation during equipment maintenance.
- Available in various sizes and configurations to meet installation requirements.
- Suitable for a wide range of industrial pneumatic applications.

**Applications**

- Industrial pneumatic systems.
- Compressed air distribution systems.
- Instrumentation and control installations.
- Industrial machinery and equipment.
- Utility systems in manufacturing facilities and power plants.
