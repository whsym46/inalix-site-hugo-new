# Ashcroft Pressure Gauge

## Short Description

The Ashcroft Pressure Gauge is a pressure measurement instrument designed to monitor liquid and gas pressure across a wide range of industrial applications. Available in various pressure ranges, dial sizes, and materials, it supports reliable pressure monitoring in process systems and industrial equipment.

## Product Description

The Ashcroft Pressure Gauge is a pressure measurement instrument manufactured by Ashcroft, a provider of pressure and temperature measurement and monitoring instruments for industrial applications. It is used to measure fluid pressure in piping systems, pumps, compressors, tanks, and various process equipment.

Available in multiple configurations, Ashcroft Pressure Gauges can be selected based on pressure range, dial size, case and wetted-part materials, process connection type, and operating conditions. Selected models also offer features designed for applications involving vibration, corrosive environments, or specific process requirements.

**Key Features**

- Designed for measuring liquid and gas pressure.
- Available in various pressure ranges and dial sizes.
- Multiple material and process connection options for different installation requirements.
- Selected models are available for specialized industrial operating conditions.
- Supports pressure monitoring across industrial systems and process equipment.

**Applications**

- Oil and gas industries.
- Chemical and petrochemical processing.
- Piping and pumping systems.
- Compressors and compressed air systems.
- Power generation and manufacturing facilities.
- Industrial utilities and process systems.

**Technical Specifications**

Specifications such as pressure range, accuracy, dial diameter, case material, wetted-part materials, connection type, and temperature range depend on the selected model. Instrument selection should consider fluid characteristics, operating pressure, environmental conditions, and installation requirements.
