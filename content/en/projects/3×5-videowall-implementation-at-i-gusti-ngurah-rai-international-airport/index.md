**Large-Scale Integrated Visual Display Solution**

INALIX implemented a 3×5 videowall system at I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport as an integrated visual display solution for presenting information and digital content.

The videowall combines multiple display units into a unified large-format screen, providing greater visibility and enabling information to be presented clearly and effectively within the airport environment.

Through reliable display infrastructure and seamless system integration, the implementation provides a flexible visual communication platform while contributing to a modern and technology-driven airport environment.
