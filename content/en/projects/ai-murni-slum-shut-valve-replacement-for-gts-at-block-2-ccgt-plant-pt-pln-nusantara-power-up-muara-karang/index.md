**Enhancing Gas Turbine System Reliability**

INALIX carried out the replacement of the Slum Shut Valve in the Gas Turbine System (GTS) at Block 2 of the CCGT Power Plant, PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The work focused on replacing critical valve components within the gas turbine system to support reliable gas flow control and maintain the operational integrity of the associated equipment.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to maintaining equipment reliability, supporting operational stability, and ensuring the readiness of critical systems within the power generation facility.
