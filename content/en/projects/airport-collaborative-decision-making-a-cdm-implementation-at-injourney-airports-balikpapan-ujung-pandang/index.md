**Enhancing Airport Operations Through Collaborative Decision Making**

INALIX implemented the Airport Collaborative Decision Making (A-CDM) system at InJourney Airports Balikpapan and Ujung Pandang to strengthen coordination and information exchange among key airport stakeholders.

The system provides a shared operational environment that enables stakeholders to access relevant and timely information, improve situational awareness, and make more informed operational decisions.

Through improved data visibility and collaboration, the A-CDM implementation supports more efficient, predictable, and coordinated airport operations.
