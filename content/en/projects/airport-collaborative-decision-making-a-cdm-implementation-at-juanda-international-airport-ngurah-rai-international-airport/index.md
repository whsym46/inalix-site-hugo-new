**Enhancing Airport Operations Through Collaborative Decision Making**

INALIX implemented the Airport Collaborative Decision Making (A-CDM) system at Juanda International Airport and Ngurah Rai International Airport to strengthen collaboration and coordination among key airport stakeholders.

The system provides a shared operational environment that facilitates timely information exchange, improves situational awareness, and enables stakeholders to make more informed and coordinated operational decisions.

Through enhanced data visibility and collaboration, the A-CDM implementation supports more efficient, predictable, and synchronized airport operations while contributing to improved overall operational performance.
