**Centralized Airport Operational Data Management**

INALIX implemented the Airport Operational Database (AODB) at InJourney Airports Sepinggan as a centralized platform for managing and distributing critical airport operational data.

The AODB serves as a central data source that enables connected systems and operational stakeholders to access consistent and up-to-date information, supporting seamless data exchange across the airport environment.

Through centralized data management and system integration, the implementation enhances operational visibility, coordination, and efficiency while providing a reliable data foundation for day-to-day airport operations.
