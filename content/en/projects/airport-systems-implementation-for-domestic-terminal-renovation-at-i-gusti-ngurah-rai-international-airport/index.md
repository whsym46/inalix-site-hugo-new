**Integrated Airport Systems for Terminal Modernization**

As part of the Domestic Terminal Renovation at I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport, INALIX implemented a comprehensive suite of airport systems designed to support integrated terminal operations and passenger information services.

The project scope included INALIX-developed Flight Information Display System (FIDS), Airport Announcement System (AAS), Airport Operational Database (AODB), SIOPSKOM, and Billing System, along with the integration of Master Clock and Digital Signage.

Through the integration of these systems, operational data can be centrally managed and distributed to support real-time flight information, passenger announcements, time synchronization, digital content distribution, and other essential terminal operations.

This implementation demonstrates INALIX’s capability to develop and integrate airport technology solutions, creating a more connected, reliable, and efficient operational environment.
