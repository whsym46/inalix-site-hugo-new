**Centralized Visual Monitoring for Airport Operations**

INALIX implemented an integrated videowall system for the Airport Operations Control Center (AOCC) at Yogyakarta International Airport (YIA).

The videowall provides a centralized visual platform for displaying operational information and supporting real-time monitoring within the control center. It enables airport operators to access relevant information more effectively and maintain greater situational awareness across airport operations.

Through an integrated and reliable visual monitoring infrastructure, the implementation supports more effective operational coordination, faster information access, and efficient decision-making within the AOCC.
