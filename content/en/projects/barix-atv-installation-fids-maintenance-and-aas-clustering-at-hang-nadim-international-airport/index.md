**Integrated Airport Information & Communication System Enhancement**

INALIX delivered the installation of Barix and ATV equipment, FIDS maintenance, and AAS clustering at Hang Nadim International Airport as part of an integrated enhancement of the airport’s information and communication infrastructure.

FIDS maintenance was carried out to maintain system reliability and ensure continuous delivery of flight information, while AAS clustering was implemented to optimize the distribution and management of airport announcements.

The installation of Barix and ATV equipment further supports the airport’s communication and information infrastructure, contributing to a more reliable and integrated operational environment.

Together, these works enhance system availability, passenger information delivery, and the continuity of day-to-day airport operations.
