**Enhancing Surveillance System Reliability**

INALIX delivered the procurement and normalization of the CCTV system at PJB-UP Muara Tawar to improve the reliability and performance of the existing surveillance infrastructure.

The project involved the provision of required CCTV equipment, restoration of existing system functionality, and optimization of the surveillance infrastructure to ensure effective and continuous monitoring across designated areas.

Through system normalization and infrastructure enhancement, the implementation provides a more reliable surveillance platform to support security, monitoring, and day-to-day operational activities.
