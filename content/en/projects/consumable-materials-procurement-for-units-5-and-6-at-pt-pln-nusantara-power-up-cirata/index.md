**Material Procurement Support for Power Plant Operational Reliability**

INALIX carried out the procurement of consumable materials for Units 5 and 6 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Cirata.

This procurement project addressed the material requirements supporting the operation and maintenance of the power generation units. The availability of consumable materials that meet technical specifications and operational requirements plays an important role in maintaining equipment readiness and supporting maintenance activities.

Through the supply of required materials, INALIX contributes to maintaining equipment availability and ensuring the continuity of power plant operations.
