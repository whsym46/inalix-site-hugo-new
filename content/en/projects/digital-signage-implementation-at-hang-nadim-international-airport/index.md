**Integrated Digital Signage for Airport Communication**

INALIX implemented a Digital Signage system at Hang Nadim International Airport to enhance visual communication and digital content delivery across the terminal.

The system enables information and digital content to be centrally managed and distributed across designated display points, providing greater flexibility in delivering relevant content to passengers and airport visitors.

Through reliable display infrastructure and centralized content management, the implementation provides an effective visual communication platform while contributing to a more modern and digitally connected airport environment.
