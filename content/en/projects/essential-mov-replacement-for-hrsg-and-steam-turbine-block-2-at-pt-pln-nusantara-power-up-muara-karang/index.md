**Enhancing Critical Valve System Reliability**

INALIX carried out the replacement of Motor Operated Valves (MOVs) associated with the essential systems of the Heat Recovery Steam Generator (HRSG) and Steam Turbine (ST) at Block 2 of PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The project focused on replacing critical valve equipment to support reliable operation and maintain the performance of essential systems within the power generation unit.

Through this work, INALIX contributed to maintaining equipment readiness, supporting operational continuity, and enhancing the reliability of critical power plant infrastructure.
