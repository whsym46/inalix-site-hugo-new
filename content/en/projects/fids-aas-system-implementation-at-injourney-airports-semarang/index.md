**Integrated Passenger Information System**

INALIX implemented FIDS and AAS as an integrated solution to enhance passenger information services at InJourney Airports Semarang.

The FIDS provides accurate and real-time flight information across designated airport displays, helping passengers stay informed about departures, arrivals, boarding gates, and flight status.

The AAS complements the system by enabling centralized and efficient airport announcements, ensuring important information can be communicated clearly throughout the terminal.

Together, these systems provide a reliable information infrastructure that supports smoother airport operations and a better passenger experience.
