**Integrated Passenger Information & Announcement System**

INALIX implemented the Flight Information Display System (FIDS) and Airport Announcement System (AAS) at Yogyakarta International Airport as an integrated solution for passenger information and airport communication.

FIDS delivers accurate and up-to-date flight information across designated display points, providing passengers with essential information related to departures, arrivals, boarding gates, and flight status.

AAS complements the system through centralized management and distribution of airport announcements, ensuring important information can be communicated clearly and consistently across the terminal.

Together, FIDS and AAS provide an integrated information and communication infrastructure that supports efficient airport operations and enhances the overall passenger experience.
