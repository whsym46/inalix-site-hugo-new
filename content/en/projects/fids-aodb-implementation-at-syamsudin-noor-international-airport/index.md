**Integrated Flight Information & Operational Data System**

INALIX implemented the Flight Information Display System (FIDS) and Airport Operational Database (AODB) at Syamsudin Noor International Airport as an integrated solution for passenger information and airport operational data management.

The AODB serves as a centralized data platform for managing and distributing operational flight information, while FIDS delivers accurate and up-to-date flight information across designated passenger areas.

Through the integration of FIDS and AODB, the implementation enables consistent data distribution, improves operational visibility, and supports reliable passenger information services and efficient day-to-day airport operations.
