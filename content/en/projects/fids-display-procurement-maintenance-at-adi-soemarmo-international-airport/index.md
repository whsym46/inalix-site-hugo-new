**Reliable Flight Information Display Infrastructure**

INALIX delivered the procurement and maintenance of FIDS monitors at Adi Soemarmo International Airport to support reliable flight information delivery throughout the terminal.

The project included the provision of display equipment as well as maintenance activities to ensure optimal performance, availability, and continuity of the FIDS display infrastructure.

Through reliable and well-maintained display systems, the implementation supports accurate passenger information delivery and contributes to efficient day-to-day airport operations.
