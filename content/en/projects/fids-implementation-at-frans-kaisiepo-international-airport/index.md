**Reliable Real-Time Flight Information**

INALIX implemented the Flight Information Display System (FIDS) at Frans Kaisiepo International Airport to enhance passenger information services across the terminal.

The system provides accurate and up-to-date flight information through designated display points, including departures, arrivals, boarding gates, and flight status.

Through reliable system infrastructure and centralized information distribution, the FIDS implementation supports effective passenger communication while contributing to smooth and efficient day-to-day airport operations.
