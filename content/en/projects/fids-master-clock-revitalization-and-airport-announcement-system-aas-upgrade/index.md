**Airport Information & Communication System Revitalization**

INALIX carried out the revitalization of the FIDS and Master Clock, along with an upgrade of the Airport Announcement System (AAS), to improve the performance and reliability of the airport’s information infrastructure.

The FIDS revitalization enhances the delivery of real-time flight information to passengers, while the Master Clock ensures consistent time synchronization across connected systems and devices.

The AAS upgrade further strengthens passenger communication by improving the airport’s capability to deliver clear and effective announcements throughout the terminal.

Together, these improvements provide a more integrated and reliable information ecosystem that supports efficient airport operations and an enhanced passenger experience.
