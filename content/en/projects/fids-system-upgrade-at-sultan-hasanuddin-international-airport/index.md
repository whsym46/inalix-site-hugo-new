**Enhancing Flight Information System Performance**

INALIX carried out the Flight Information Display System (FIDS) upgrade at Sultan Hasanuddin International Airport to improve the performance and reliability of the airport’s passenger information infrastructure.

The project involved upgrading and optimizing the existing FIDS environment to enhance system availability and ensure accurate, up-to-date flight information is consistently distributed across designated display points.

Through this system upgrade, FIDS provides a more reliable platform for passenger information delivery while supporting efficient and seamless day-to-day airport operations.
