**Large-Scale Flight Information Display Solution**

INALIX delivered the procurement and installation of the FIDS Videowall at I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport to enhance the presentation and accessibility of flight information within the terminal.

The videowall provides a large-format visual platform for displaying real-time flight information, enabling passengers to access essential departure, arrival, and flight status information more clearly and efficiently.

Through reliable display infrastructure and seamless integration with the FIDS environment, the implementation supports effective passenger information delivery and contributes to a modern and efficient airport experience.
