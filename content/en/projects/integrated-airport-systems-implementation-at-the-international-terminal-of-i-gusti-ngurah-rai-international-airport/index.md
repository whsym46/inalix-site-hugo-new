**Integrated Airport Systems for International Terminal Operations**

INALIX implemented a comprehensive suite of airport systems at the International Terminal of I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport to support integrated airport operations and passenger services.

The project scope includes INALIX-developed Flight Information Display System (FIDS), Airport Announcement System (AAS), Airport Operational Database (AODB), SIOPSKOM, and Billing System, along with Digital Signage as part of the terminal’s integrated information infrastructure.

Through seamless system integration, the implementation enables centralized operational data management, real-time flight information distribution, passenger announcements, operational coordination, billing processes, and digital content delivery across the terminal.

The project demonstrates INALIX’s capability to develop and integrate comprehensive airport technology solutions, creating a connected, reliable, and efficient technology ecosystem for complex international terminal operations.
