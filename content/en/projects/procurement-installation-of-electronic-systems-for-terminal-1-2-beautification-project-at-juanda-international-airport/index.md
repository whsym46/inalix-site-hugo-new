**Integrated Electronic Systems for Terminal Enhancement**

INALIX delivered the procurement and installation of electronic systems as part of the Terminal 1 & 2 Beautification Project at Juanda International Airport.

The project involved the supply, installation, and integration of electronic infrastructure across designated terminal areas, with a focus on system reliability, operational functionality, and seamless integration with the enhanced terminal environment.

Through the integration of technology and functional infrastructure, the project contributes to a more modern, efficient, and passenger-friendly airport environment.
