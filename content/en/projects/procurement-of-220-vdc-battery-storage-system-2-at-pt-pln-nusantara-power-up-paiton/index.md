**Reliable Backup Power for Critical Electrical Systems**

INALIX supports the procurement of the 220 VDC Battery Storage System #2 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Paiton.

The battery storage system serves as a backup DC power source to maintain the availability of electrical supply for critical equipment, including control and protection systems. Its reliability is essential to supporting safe and continuous power plant operations.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to strengthening the reliability of the plant's DC power infrastructure and supporting operational continuity.
