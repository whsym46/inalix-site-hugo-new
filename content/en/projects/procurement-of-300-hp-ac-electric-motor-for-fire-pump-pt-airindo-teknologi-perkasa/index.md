**Reliable Electric Motor Solution for Fire Protection Systems**

INALIX supports the procurement of a 300 HP AC electric motor for a fire pump for PT Airindo Teknologi Perkasa.

Designed to drive high-capacity fire pump equipment, the electric motor plays a critical role in delivering the power required for fire protection operations. Its reliable performance is essential to maintaining system readiness and supporting emergency response requirements.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to providing reliable electrical equipment solutions for critical fire protection infrastructure.
