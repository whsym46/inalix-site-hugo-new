**Supporting Reliable Actuator and Valve Operations**

INALIX supports the procurement of actuator inner parts for PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

Actuator internal components are essential to the proper functioning of automated valve systems, enabling controlled and reliable valve operation. Maintaining these components helps support equipment performance and operational continuity.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to meeting power plant maintenance requirements and supporting the reliability of operational equipment.
