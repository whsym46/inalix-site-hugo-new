**Enhancing Fire Protection System Readiness and Reliability**

INALIX supports the procurement of an additional jockey pump and diesel fire pump at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Cirata.

The jockey pump helps maintain pressure within the fire protection piping network, while the diesel fire pump provides an independent water supply for firefighting operations when required.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to strengthening fire protection infrastructure and supporting operational safety at the power plant.
