**Enhancing Electrical Power Supply Reliability**

INALIX carried out the procurement of one battery bank and charger system for Unit 2, Phase 2 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Kaltim Teluk.

The battery and charger system plays an important role in providing a reliable DC power source for essential electrical and control equipment, particularly when the primary power supply is unavailable.

Through this procurement, INALIX supports the availability of backup power infrastructure, enhances electrical system reliability, and contributes to maintaining operational continuity at the power generation facility.
