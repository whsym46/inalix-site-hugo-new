**Supporting Emergency Power System Reliability**

INALIX carried out the procurement of a battery charger and battery storage system for EDG Phase 1 at Block 1 of the CCGT Power Plant at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The battery storage and charger system provides the DC power required to support the Emergency Diesel Generator (EDG), particularly during emergency conditions when reliable backup power is essential.

Through this procurement, INALIX contributes to maintaining the readiness of the emergency power system, supporting reliable generator operation, and enhancing the overall reliability of the power generation facility.
