**Reliable DC Power Supply for Power Plant Operations**

INALIX supports the procurement of a battery storage and battery charger system at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The battery storage system provides backup DC power, while the battery charger maintains battery capacity and readiness. Together, these components play an important role in supporting control systems, protection equipment, and other critical electrical infrastructure.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to maintaining DC power system reliability and supporting the continuity of power plant operations.
