**Enhancing Generator Electrical System Reliability**

INALIX carried out the procurement of a battery storage and battery charger system for Gas Turbine Generator (GTG) 1.1 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The battery and charger system provides a reliable DC power source to support generator starting, control systems, and essential auxiliary equipment, helping ensure operational readiness and dependable system performance.

Through this procurement, INALIX contributes to maintaining the availability of critical electrical infrastructure and supporting the operational reliability of the power generation facility.
