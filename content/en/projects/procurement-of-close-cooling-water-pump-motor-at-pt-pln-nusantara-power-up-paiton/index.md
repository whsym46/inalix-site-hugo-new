**Supporting Cooling Water System Reliability**

INALIX carried out the procurement of an electric motor for the Close Cooling Water Pump at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Paiton.

The motor serves as the prime mover for the pump, supporting the circulation of cooling water required by various auxiliary systems and equipment within the power plant.

Through this procurement, INALIX contributes to maintaining equipment availability, supporting cooling system performance, and ensuring the operational reliability of the power generation facility.
