**Supporting Reliable Condensate System Operations**

INALIX supports the procurement of a condensate pump for Unit 1 at Bangka Steam Power Plant as part of its commitment to providing reliable equipment solutions for power generation facilities.

The condensate pump is a critical component in the steam-water cycle, responsible for transferring condensate from the condenser to the feedwater system. Its reliable operation helps maintain continuous circulation and supports the overall performance of the power generation process.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to supporting the reliability of plant auxiliary systems and the continuity of power generation operations.
