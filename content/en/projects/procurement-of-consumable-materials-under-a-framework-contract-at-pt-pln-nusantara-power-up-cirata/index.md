**Supporting Operational Continuity Through Reliable Material Supply**

INALIX supports the procurement of consumable materials under a framework contract at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Cirata.

The supply of consumable materials plays an important role in meeting routine maintenance requirements and supporting various operational activities within the power plant.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to ensuring material availability and supporting the continuity and reliability of power plant operations.
