**Enhancing Transformer Monitoring and Early Warning Capabilities**

INALIX supports the procurement of EWS Hydran sensors for main transformers ST 2.0, ST 3.0, and GTG 3.3 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Gresik.

Hydran sensors monitor dissolved gases in transformer insulating oil to help detect early indications of potential internal faults. This provides valuable information for operational assessment and maintenance planning.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to strengthening transformer condition monitoring and supporting the reliability of power generation equipment.
