**Supporting Gas Turbine Ignition System Reliability**

INALIX carried out the procurement of an igniter transformer for the Gas Turbine system at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The igniter transformer is a critical component of the gas turbine ignition system, responsible for generating the high-voltage output required to produce an ignition spark during the turbine starting sequence.

Through this procurement, INALIX contributes to maintaining ignition system reliability, supporting generator starting readiness, and ensuring the operational availability of critical gas turbine equipment.
