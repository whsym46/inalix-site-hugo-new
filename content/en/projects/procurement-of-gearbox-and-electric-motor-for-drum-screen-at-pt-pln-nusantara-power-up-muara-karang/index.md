**Supporting Drum Screen System Reliability**

INALIX carried out the procurement of a gearbox and electric motor for the Drum Screen system at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The gearbox and electric motor serve as essential drive components that support the mechanical operation of the drum screen equipment. Their availability is important to maintain equipment functionality and ensure reliable system performance.

Through this procurement, INALIX contributes to supporting equipment readiness, maintaining operational efficiency, and enhancing the reliability of supporting systems within the power generation facility.
