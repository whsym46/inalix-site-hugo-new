**Supporting Mill System Performance and Reliability**

INALIX carried out the procurement of a gearbox for the mill system at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Paiton.

The gearbox serves as a critical mechanical component responsible for transmitting power and providing the required torque to drive the milling equipment. Its proper operation is essential to maintaining the performance and reliability of the milling system.

Through this procurement, INALIX contributes to ensuring equipment availability, supporting efficient milling operations, and maintaining the operational reliability of the power generation facility.
