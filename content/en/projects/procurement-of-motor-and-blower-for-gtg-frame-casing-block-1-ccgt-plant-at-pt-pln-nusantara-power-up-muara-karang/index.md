**Supporting GTG Ventilation and Cooling System Reliability**

INALIX supports the procurement of a motor and blower for the GTG frame casing at Block 1 CCGT Plant, PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The motor drives the blower to circulate air within the gas turbine frame casing, helping maintain suitable operating conditions and support equipment reliability.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to supporting the performance of auxiliary equipment and the operational continuity of the power plant.
