**Supporting Reliable MOV Operations in Power Plant Systems**

INALIX supports the procurement of Motor Operated Valve (MOV) materials for the ID Fan and Turbine areas of Unit 20 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Rembang.

MOV components play an important role in controlling fluid flow through automated valve operation. Maintaining the condition of these components helps support equipment performance and operational reliability.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to supporting the maintenance requirements and operational continuity of the power plant.
