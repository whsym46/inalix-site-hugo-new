**Supporting Overhaul Activities Through Reliable Material Supply**

INALIX carried out the procurement of consumable materials for the 2025 overhaul of Units 3 and 4 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Cirata.

The project focused on supplying materials required to support scheduled overhaul and maintenance activities. The availability of appropriate consumables is essential to facilitate maintenance work, maintain equipment condition, and support the operational readiness of the generating units.

Through this procurement project, INALIX contributed to supporting maintenance requirements and the reliability of power generation operations.
