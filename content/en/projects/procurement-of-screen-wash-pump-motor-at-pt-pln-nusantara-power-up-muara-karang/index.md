**Reliable Motor Solution for Screen Washing Systems**

INALIX supports the procurement of a screen wash pump motor at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

The motor plays an essential role in driving the screen wash pump, ensuring adequate water circulation for the cleaning process. Reliable motor performance helps maintain the effectiveness of the washing system and supports the operational reliability of associated equipment.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to providing reliable electrical equipment solutions to support power plant operations.
