**Reliable Backup Power for Essential Plant Systems**

INALIX supports the procurement of storage batteries for STG #1.0 and the Lobby, Phase 1 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Karang.

Storage batteries play an important role in providing backup DC power to support critical electrical equipment and maintain system availability during power interruptions. Their reliability is essential to supporting safe and continuous plant operations.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to strengthening the plant's electrical infrastructure and supporting operational continuity.
