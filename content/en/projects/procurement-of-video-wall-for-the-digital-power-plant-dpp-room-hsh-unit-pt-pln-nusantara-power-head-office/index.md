**Integrated Visual Monitoring for Digital Power Plant Operations**

INALIX supports the procurement of a video wall for the Digital Power Plant (DPP) Room at the HSH Unit of PT PLN Nusantara Power Head Office.

The video wall provides a centralized display platform for presenting operational data, monitoring dashboards, and other relevant information. This solution helps operators access and review information more efficiently.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to strengthening digital monitoring capabilities and supporting more integrated power plant operations.
