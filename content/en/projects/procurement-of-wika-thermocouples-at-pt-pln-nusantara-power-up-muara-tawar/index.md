**Reliable Temperature Measurement for Power Plant Operations**

INALIX supports the procurement of WIKA thermocouples at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Muara Tawar.

Thermocouples are essential temperature measurement instruments that convert temperature variations into electrical signals for monitoring and control systems. Their accurate and reliable readings help operators monitor equipment conditions and support operational safety.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to providing reliable instrumentation solutions for power plant monitoring and control applications.
