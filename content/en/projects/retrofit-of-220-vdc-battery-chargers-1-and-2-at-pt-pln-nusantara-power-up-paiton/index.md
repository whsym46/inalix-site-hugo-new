**Enhancing the Reliability of the 220 VDC Battery Charger System**

INALIX supports the retrofit of 220 VDC Battery Chargers #1 and #2 at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Paiton as part of ongoing efforts to maintain and improve the reliability of the power plant's electrical infrastructure.

The Battery Charger is a critical component of the plant's DC system, ensuring the availability of power for control equipment, protection systems, and other supporting facilities.

Through this project, INALIX contributes to maintaining electrical system reliability and supporting the operational continuity of the power plant.
