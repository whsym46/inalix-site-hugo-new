**Enhancing Airport Operational & Communication Systems**

INALIX carried out the SIOPSKOM system upgrade at Hang Nadim International Airport to improve the performance and reliability of the airport’s operational and communication infrastructure.

The project involved upgrading and optimizing the existing system to enhance its capabilities, improve operational visibility, and ensure more reliable information management and communication across airport operations.

Through this upgrade, SIOPSKOM provides a more robust and efficient platform to support operational coordination and the continuity of day-to-day airport activities.
