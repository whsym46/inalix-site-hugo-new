**Professional Voice Recording for Airport Announcement Systems**

INALIX provided professional supervision for voice recording sessions as part of the development of the Automatic Announcement System (AAS) sound database.

The process focused on maintaining consistency in pronunciation, voice delivery, clarity, and overall audio quality across recorded announcement segments. Each recording was prepared to support structured integration into the AAS database for automated passenger announcements.

Through a standardized recording and quality control process, the project provides a reliable audio foundation for delivering clear, consistent, and professional announcements across airport environments.
