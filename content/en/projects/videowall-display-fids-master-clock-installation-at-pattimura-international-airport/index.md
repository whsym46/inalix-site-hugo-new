**Integrated Passenger Information & Display Systems**

INALIX delivered the installation and integration of Videowall Display, Flight Information Display System (FIDS), and Master Clock at Pattimura International Airport.

The FIDS enables accurate and timely flight information delivery across passenger areas, while the Videowall provides a centralized visual communication platform for displaying relevant airport information and content.

The Master Clock ensures consistent time synchronization across connected systems and devices throughout the airport environment.

Together, these systems provide an integrated information infrastructure that enhances passenger communication, supports operational efficiency, and contributes to a more reliable airport experience.
