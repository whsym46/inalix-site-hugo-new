**Centralized Visual Monitoring for Control Center Operations**

INALIX implemented an integrated videowall system for Bank BRI Control Center to enhance centralized monitoring and information visualization.

The videowall provides a large-format visual platform for displaying monitoring data and operational information, enabling operators to access relevant information more effectively and maintain greater situational awareness.

Through reliable display infrastructure and seamless system integration, the implementation supports more effective monitoring, operational coordination, and decision-making within the control center environment.
