**Integrated Large-Scale Visual Display Solution**

INALIX implemented an integrated videowall system at SBU, I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport, as a large-scale visual platform for displaying information and digital content.

The videowall combines multiple display units into a unified screen, providing enhanced visibility and enabling information to be presented clearly and effectively within the designated area.

Through reliable display infrastructure and seamless system integration, the implementation provides an effective visual communication platform while supporting a modern and technology-driven airport environment.
