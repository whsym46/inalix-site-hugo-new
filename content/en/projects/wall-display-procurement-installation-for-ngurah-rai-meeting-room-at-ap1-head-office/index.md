**Integrated Visual Display for Collaborative Meeting Environment**

INALIX delivered the procurement and installation of a wall display system for the Ngurah Rai Meeting Room at AP1 Head Office.

The system provides a large-format visual platform for presentations, data visualization, and information sharing, enabling meeting participants to access and review content clearly and effectively.

Through reliable display infrastructure and seamless system integration, the implementation enhances the meeting room’s visual communication capabilities and supports more effective collaboration, presentations, and decision-making.
