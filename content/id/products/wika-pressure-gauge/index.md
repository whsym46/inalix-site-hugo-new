WIKA Pressure Gauge is a pressure measurement instrument manufactured by WIKA, a German-based manufacturer of industrial measurement technology. Designed for a wide range of industrial applications, it enables operators to monitor system pressure through a clear and easy-to-read dial.

Suitable for use in piping systems, pumps, tanks, and industrial machinery, WIKA Pressure Gauge supports reliable pressure monitoring and helps maintain efficient and safe operations.

**Key Features:**

- Designed for measuring liquid and gas pressure.
- Clear, easy-to-read analog display.
- Available in various pressure ranges and dial sizes.
- Multiple material and connection options depending on the model.
- Suitable for a wide range of industrial applications.

**Applications:**

- Industrial manufacturing and processing.
- Pumping and piping systems.
- Oil and gas facilities.
- HVAC and industrial utilities.
- General industrial pressure monitoring.
